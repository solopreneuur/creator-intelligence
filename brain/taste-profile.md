# Taste profile

A plain-English model of what interests you and why. The weekly run rewrites it
from the week's signals. You can correct it by hand at any time. About a quarter
of each run (the wildcards) ignores this file on purpose.

Last rewritten: 2026-10-31 (first weekly run; still the starting profile — the
week behind it is the launch day itself, with no reply signals yet to rewrite
it by)

## What you're after

- **Raw material from three fields, weighted equally:** marketing, behavioural
  psychology, and founders and company building. Not a psychology-study finder.
- **Two reasons to keep a find:** to know the fields better, and to supply big
  topics with fresh angles for short-form video. A find is worth keeping even if
  it never becomes a video.
- **The concept is the big topic, the find is the fresh angle.** Friction or
  identity can be well known; the specific story that anchors them has to be new
  to most people.
- **Age doesn't matter.** A 1962 campaign and last week's episode compete on the
  same terms: richness and novelty.

## What lands

- A **familiar hook**: something people recognise in a second (a sound, a
  product, a brand, a habit), with a hidden reason behind it. The Uber chime is
  the model.
- A **real surprise**: a number, a reversal, or an origin that's not what you'd
  expect.
- A **principle that travels**: an idea underneath that applies far beyond the
  story, ideally one with a name.
- **Instantly understandable.** If it takes a paragraph of setup, it's too
  obscure. Odd is good, bizarre isn't.
- **Concrete over abstract**: named companies, named people, real numbers,
  specific objects.
- **Popular companies are welcome** when the story is a specific, little-known
  detail, not the generic one ("how Apple scaled").

## What doesn't

- Stories told everywhere (the marshmallow test, Netflix vs Blockbuster, the jam
  study, Airbnb's cereal boxes).
- Hedged, nuanced framing. Volume over depth.
- Finds so obscure or strange they don't make sense without context.
- Cards that steer toward a take. The card describes; the take is yours.
- Quotes without a real clip. Invented explanations for "hidden reason" finds.

## Accuracy

Not a priority unless something is blatantly wrong. A source link on every card
is the check. Quotes are the exception: real person, real clip, timestamp.
